Note to co-authors: CV update in the FAVRI model (25 Oct 2026, branch fix-decisionsupport-units)

WHAT WAS WRONG
The coefficient of variation (CV_value) was entered as 0.1 to 0.4. The decisionSupport
function vv() reads CV as percent, so the model used 0.1% to 0.4% instead of 10% to 40%.
Year to year variation in cash flows was therefore close to zero.
All other inputs were checked and are correct (discount rate and inflation in percent,
chances and proportions on a 0 to 1 scale).

WHAT I CHANGED
1. CV_value set to 10 to 40 (posnorm).
2. Input table labels: 0 to 1 inputs now say "probability 0-1" or "proportion 0-1" instead of "%".
   Also fixed one copy-paste error and one typo in descriptions. No effect on results.
3. Renamed prob_increased_farmer_reputation to farmer_reputation_revenue_uplift.
   It is a multiplier (1 + x), not a probability. No effect on results.
4. Removed an old unused copy of the model (company_function.R) and an outdated
   results file (results/company_simulation_results.csv, from July 2025).
5. Re-ran the model (seed 84, 10,000 runs), EVPI and all figures.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT
The decision does not change. Means are unchanged; the spread is slightly wider.
